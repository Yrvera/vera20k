# Checkpoints

For sustained work, replace one concise current checkpoint; do not append a diary.
Record worktree/branch/HEAD, unmerged or dirty work, literal validation and coverage,
review/PR state, blockers/residuals, scope/authority amendments and next safe action.
Reconcile actual Git/process state on resumption.

At planned pauses finish operations and leave no merge or owned Cargo pending.
On an immediate stop, start no tests, investigation or cleanup; preserve work, stop
assigning workers and report ongoing operations. Never kill a compile or another
task's process. Describe incomplete work honestly; do not rerun checks to beautify
the handoff. Small completed tasks need only their final result.
