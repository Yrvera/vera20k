# Design Review

Review the named or contextually established design under
[ENGINE.md](../../../ENGINE.md) and [independent review](../_shared/review.md).
The author's packet does not limit inquiry into original evidence or omitted scope.

Assess whether current owners/consumers and native evidence support the approach,
its applicability and exclusions. Check authority handoffs, migrations, retiring
old logic and whether validation exercises real production behavior. Visual designs
need the parent composition and active frames/flags, not merely a draw helper.

Report readiness, actionable findings and important coverage gaps. Judge substance,
not template headings or architectural preferences. Leave files unchanged for
review-only requests; authorized design corrections do not authorize implementation.
