## Deprioritized For This Swarm

- Air, jumpjet, hover, teleport, tunnel, and ship locomotors: distinct systems,
  but they would dilute the reported normal ground-unit and MCV problem.
- MCV deploy/undeploy: deployment is a separate mission/state transition; the
  reported symptom is ordinary movement before deployment.
- Broad "all movement" tracing: too abstract for literal value comparison. The
  three HIGH scenarios above isolate DriveLocomotion, dynamic repathing, and
  WalkLocomotion while sharing simple stock terrain fixtures.
