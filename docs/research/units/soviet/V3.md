# V3 — V3 Rocket Launcher

**Side classification:** Soviet (Owner=Russians,Confederation,Africans,Arabs).
**Role:** Long-range siege artillery. Spawns a single `V3ROCKET` (aircraft-class
missile) per attack via the `SpawnManagerClass` mechanism. The rocket flies to the
target and detonates with a hardcoded warhead. Once fired, the V3 is "out of ammo"
(`SpawnReloadRate=0` — spawn doesn't come back) and switches to the `V3WO` (V3
WithOut-rocket) voxel until `SpawnRegenRate=400` ticks regenerate a new rocket.

> Output bar: spawn-regen cadence, rocket flight curve, NoSpawnAlt voxel swap timing,
> and the V3WO-vs-V3 visual transition must all match gamemd exactly. Players watch
> the back of the V3 voxel to know when the next rocket is ready.

> **Deep-RE cross-references — DO NOT re-derive:**
> - **[SPAWN_MANAGER_CLASS_GHIDRA_REPORT.md](../../SPAWN_MANAGER_CLASS_GHIDRA_REPORT.md)** — 116-byte SpawnManagerClass at `0x006B6C90`, full 7-state state machine, AI dispatched from `TechnoClass::AI_Update @ 0x006F9E50` via `vtable+0x5C`. The V3 is one of 5 verified YR consumers.
> - **[ROCKET_LOCOMOTION_CLASS_GHIDRA_REPORT.md](../../ROCKET_LOCOMOTION_CLASS_GHIDRA_REPORT.md)** — RocketLocomotionClass (CLSID `B7B49766-...`) used by V3ROCKET. Handles the missile's takeoff, mid-flight, and target-strike phases.

> Ghidra confirms no `"V3"` plain string in `gamemd.exe` for the unit ID — all behavior
> is generic flag-driven via `Spawns=V3ROCKET` + the SpawnManagerClass mechanism.

### `V3WO.VXL` — the "out of ammo" voxel

The engine loads `V3WO.VXL` instead of `V3.VXL` when the spawn pool is empty. The
`WO` voxel shows the V3 truck **without** the missile on its back — the rear platform
is empty. As soon as `SpawnRegenRate=400` ticks elapse and a new rocket is regenerated,
the engine swaps back to `V3.VXL` (missile visible on the truck).

This is purely visual — gameplay is governed by the SpawnManagerClass state. But the
visual swap is parity-critical: the player relies on it to know when the V3 is ready
to fire again.

## 3. Weapons — `[V3Launcher]` (the spawner) + `[V3Cluster]` (unused per comment)

### `[V3Launcher]` (V3's Primary)

```ini
[V3Launcher]
Damage=1
ROF=150
Range=18
MinimumRange=5
Spawner=yes
Projectile=InvisibleHigh
Speed=10
Warhead=Special
```

| Key | Value | Effect |
|-----|-------|--------|
| `Damage` | `1` | **Placeholder** — the weapon itself does 1 damage (negligible). The real damage comes from the spawned V3ROCKET detonating on target. |
| `ROF` | `150` | 150-tick cooldown between fire orders. **Shorter than `SpawnRegenRate=400`** — so the rate-limit is actually the spawn regen, not the weapon ROF. |
| `Range` | `18` | **18-cell range** — the longest weapon range in the game. V3 can fire far across the map. Compared to V3's `Sight=7`, the V3 fires "blind" beyond its own vision — requires friendly spotters. |
| `MinimumRange` | `5` | **Cannot fire at targets within 5 cells.** V3 has a "dead zone" — close enemies are immune to V3 fire. Forces enemies to close range as a counter-tactic. |
| `Spawner` | `yes` | WeaponType (verified — 0x00849538 → WeaponTypeClass__ReadINI @ 0x007720ed). **Hardcoded flag**: when set, the weapon's "fire" action triggers `SpawnManagerClass::Spawn_One` (or equivalent) instead of creating a projectile bullet. The weapon's Damage and Warhead are placeholders. The spawned unit (V3ROCKET) handles the actual damage on impact. |
| `Projectile` | `InvisibleHigh` | Inviso, SubjectToElevation=yes. Not actually used (Spawner=yes overrides), but required for parser. |
| `Speed` | `10` | Placeholder. |
| `Warhead` | `Special` | Placeholder warhead — see §4. |

## 6. Prerequisites / owners / availability

- **Prerequisite**: `NAWEAP,NARADR` — Soviet War Factory + Radar.
- **TechLevel** = `3`.
- **Owner**: 4 Soviet countries.
- **`CrateGoodie=yes`** — can drop from crates.
- **Cost** = $800.
- **`AllowedToStartInMultiplayer=no`** — not preplaced.

### Strategic role

V3 is the Soviet **early-game artillery siege** unit. Key characteristics:
- **18-cell range** vs Sight=7 → requires spotters (e.g., Yak/MIG observers, Terror Drones to scout).
- **MinimumRange=5** → must be kept at distance; vulnerable to fast melee units.
- **One-shot per 400 ticks** (~6.7s) — slow rate of fire, but each rocket deals heavy damage.
- **Spawns aircraft missile** — V3ROCKET flies through the air, can fly over walls/cliffs (unlike most ground weapons). This is what makes V3 effective against walled bases.
- **No anti-air** — V3 is helpless vs aircraft.
- **150 HP, light armor** — dies fast to any direct combat unit.

Compared to Allied counterparts: V3 vs Prism Tank (`SREF`) — both are tier-3 siege.
Prism Tank has shorter range but instant-hit beam; V3 has longer range but slow flying
missile. Different siege philosophies.

## 9. Veterancy

### Veteran (1 chevron) — `STRONGER, FIREPOWER, ROF, SIGHT, FASTER`
- `STRONGER` — +25% HP (150 → 187)
- `FIREPOWER` — +25% damage. **But Spawner weapons have Damage=1 placeholder** — the FIREPOWER bonus may apply to the spawned rocket's damage (or may have no effect). Unclear without binary trace.
- **`ROF`** — −25% ROF (150 → 112), but `SpawnRegenRate=400` is the binding constraint. ROF at veteran may speed up the order-to-fire pipeline but not the regen cycle. Effective fire rate is bound by SpawnRegenRate.
- `SIGHT` — +20% sight (7 → 8.4)
- `FASTER` — +20% speed (4 → 4.8)

### Elite (2 chevrons) — `SELF_HEAL, STRONGER, FIREPOWER, ROF`
- Adds `SELF_HEAL`
- Reapplies STRONGER, FIREPOWER, ROF

**No `ElitePrimary=`** — V3 keeps `V3Launcher` at all ranks. The spawner mechanism
doesn't change at elite.
