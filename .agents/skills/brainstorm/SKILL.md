# Brainstorm

Follow [ENGINE.md](../../../ENGINE.md). Establish the affected production path,
state owners and consequential native/retail evidence, then recommend an approach.
Explain alternatives when they represent a real choice.

Cover the scenario and completion condition; ownership and data/control flow;
production integration and retiring old authority; dependencies/migration;
concrete validation; and unresolved decisions. Scale detail to the task rather
than filling a template.

For visual work, establish parent draw order, active flags, asset/frame selection,
anchors, clipping and palette. A helper or loaded asset alone does not establish
what appears on screen. For gameplay, follow the surrounding loop and readers.

Save substantial/requested designs in the task's worktree at
`docs/plans/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>-design.md`, unless another location was requested.
A design-only request stops there. Existing implementation authority includes
routine design choices; continue without another approval gate. Use
[independent review](../_shared/review.md) as warranted, allowing challenges to
the approach and omitted scope.
